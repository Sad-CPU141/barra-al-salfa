Barra Al‑Salfaبرا السالفة

Coming soon

Everyone knows the secret word except one. Find the outsider before they figure it out.

How it'll work

  1. Pass the phone. Each player taps to see the secret word, or "You're out of the loop!"
  2. Ask around. Take turns asking each other questions. Be vague enough to fool the outsider, specific enough to prove you know it.
  3. Vote. Point at who you think is out of the loop.
  4. Last chance. A caught outsider can still win by guessing the word.

Planned

4 to 12 players on one phone. Arabic and English word packs (food, places, football, jobs and more), two outsiders for big groups, a round timer, and scores across rounds.
